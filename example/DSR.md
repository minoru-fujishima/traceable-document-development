# Document State Register (DSR)

## Document

- **Title / Working title:** Why the weekly meeting was moved
- **Purpose:** Explain a small operational change and the reasoning behind it.
- **Current DSR version:** v3
- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Baseline for:** `document.md`

## Current Document State

### A. Current situation

**A01.** The team currently holds a weekly meeting on Monday morning.

**A02.** Several team members have recurring external commitments on Monday mornings.

**A03.** Attendance records from the previous eight weeks show that Monday has had the lowest attendance among the weekdays considered.

- **Source:** Internal attendance record
- **Evidence:** Attendance record for the previous eight weekly meetings

### B. Decision

**B01.** The weekly meeting will be moved from Monday morning to Tuesday afternoon starting next month.

**B02.** Tuesday afternoon was selected because all current team members indicated that they can normally attend at that time.

- **Source:** Availability check with current team members

**B03.** After the schedule change, the team will continue to track attendance to determine whether participation actually improves.

### C. Scope and limitation

**C01.** The change is intended to improve attendance under the team's current schedule.

**C02.** The available evidence does not establish that Tuesday afternoon is generally the best meeting time for other teams or for future members.

## Negative Knowledge

**NK01.** “Monday morning meetings are inherently ineffective.”

- **Disposition:** Rejected
- **Reason:** The available information only shows an attendance problem for this team under its current schedule.

**NK02.** “Moving the meeting to Tuesday afternoon will improve productivity.”

- **Disposition:** Rejected
- **Reason:** No productivity data have been collected. The decision is based on expected attendance, not demonstrated productivity.

## Open / Unresolved

**U01.** Whether attendance actually improves after the schedule change.

- **Needed:** Attendance data after the change
